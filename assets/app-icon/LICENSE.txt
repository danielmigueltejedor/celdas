Celdas app icon (gridcraft.svg, gridcraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in this
directory, and the web favicon where the project has one).

The icon is a portrait of Blaise Pascal cut out, converted to black and white and placed over the
app colour. It belongs to the Nodalia icon family: one historical figure per app.

Source portrait: "Portrait of Blaise Pascal MET DP828975.jpg", from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Portrait_of_Blaise_Pascal_MET_DP828975.jpg).
Status of the source: CC0.
